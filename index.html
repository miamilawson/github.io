Why Brands Work With Us

What's holding
your brand back?

Most brands don't come to us looking for a distributor. They come to us because something in their channel strategy is broken. Here's what we usually hear first.

“Unauthorized sellers are wrecking my pricing.”
Every rogue listing costs you margin and retailer trust. We monitor MAP daily across every channel and move the moment a listing goes out of policy.
“I can't afford to sit on unsold inventory.”
We don't take product on consignment. We buy it outright, so the cash is yours on day one and the sell-through risk is ours.
“My brand looks different on every marketplace.”
Inconsistent imagery and copy kill conversion. We rebuild listings to one standard so your brand reads the same on a shelf, on Amazon, and everywhere else.
“I don't have a team to run retail distribution.”
Building one is expensive and slow. Our existing network in grocery, beauty, craft, and pet gets you placement without the headcount.

FAQ

Questions brands ask us first.

If yours isn't here, call us. We'd rather answer it directly than have you guess.

Do you buy inventory outright, or is this consignment?

Outright. We purchase your inventory and take ownership of it. You get paid at purchase and you're not waiting on sell-through to see revenue.

What categories do you work in?

Our network is strongest in grocery, beauty, craft, and pet products, with nationwide reach in those categories. If your product sits outside them, reach out anyway — we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

Do I have to give up my other sellers or channels?

No. Many brands start with a single category or channel to see how we perform before expanding. We can also work alongside your existing distribution while we help clean up unauthorized sellers.

How do you protect my pricing?

We monitor your MAP policy across marketplaces on an ongoing basis and act on violations as they appear. Since we own the inventory, protecting your price protects our margin too.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term commitment is required to start. We'd rather earn the next purchase order than lock you into one.

How quickly can we get started?

Once we've reviewed your catalog and agreed on terms, most brands move to a first purchase order within a few weeks. Call us and we'll give you a realistic timeline for your situation.
